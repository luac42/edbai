01背景Background

隨着人工智能的急速發展以及日益增加的創科人才需求，教育局於 2026 年 6 月公布《中小學數字教育發展藍圖》，包括《中小學人工智能素養學習架構》（學習架構），以及將於 2026/27 學年內公布《小學資訊與創新科技課程框架》，通過學習和應用人工智能，提升學生的人工智能素養。新課程亦包括資訊科技概念與應用、計算思維和編程，有助學生學習人工智能的運作概念，以及幫助學生應用人工智能實踐解難方案。

As artificial intelligence develops quickly and the demand for innovation and technology talent grows, the Education Bureau published the Blueprint for the Development of Digital Education in Primary and Secondary Schools in June 2026. The Blueprint includes the AI Literacy Learning Framework for Primary and Secondary Schools (the Learning Framework). During the 2026/27 school year the Bureau will also publish the Primary Information and Innovation Technology Curriculum Framework. Through learning and applying AI, students build AI literacy. The new curriculum also covers IT concepts and applications, computational thinking, and programming. These help students understand how AI works and use AI to carry out problem-solving plans.

本文件取自資訊與創新科技課程框架的人工智能部分，由「檢視小學階段科技教育專責委員會」擬訂，經過課程發展議會下的「科技教育委員會」審閱，旨在幫助學校於校本電腦課試行小學資訊與創新科技課程的人工智能部分，以落實學習架構的內容。

This document is the AI section of the Information and Innovation Technology Curriculum Framework. It was drafted by the Ad Hoc Committee on the Review of Technology Education at Primary Level and reviewed by the Committee on Technology Education under the Curriculum Development Council. It helps schools trial this AI section in school-based computer lessons, so they can put the Learning Framework into practice.

02在小學階段推展人工智能教育Promoting AI education in primary schools

學習和應用人工智能Learning and applying AI

學校應引導學生明白人工智能只是輔助工具，通過科技教育（資訊及通訊科技）的學習內容，以及於不同科目、跨學科和範疇的應用場景，鼓勵學生主動思考，培養慎思明辨的能力和正確的價值觀，避免過度依賴人工智能而形成「思維外判」。學校通過設計探究為本的學與教活動，讓學生在應用人工智能的同時，反思其限制與影響，從而建立負責任和主動的學習態度。

Schools should help students see that AI is only a supporting tool. Through technology education (information and communication technology), and through uses in different subjects, across subjects, and in different fields, schools encourage students to think for themselves, to judge carefully, and to hold sound values, so they do not depend on AI so much that they outsource their thinking. Inquiry-based learning and teaching activities let students use AI and, at the same time, reflect on its limits and effects, and so build a responsible, active approach to learning.

學習階段一Learning Stage 1

人工智能相關的學與教主要通過示範與體驗活動，引導學生認識人工智能的日常應用與基本能力。學生需要在教師的指導下初步接觸和體驗人工智能工具。教師需引導和鼓勵學生多思考，避免他們過度使用資訊科技和過早依賴人工智能。

Learning and teaching about AI is mainly through demonstration and hands-on experience, so students meet everyday uses of AI and its basic abilities. Students have a first encounter with AI tools under the teacher’s guidance. Teachers lead and encourage students to think more, so they do not overuse IT or come to depend on AI too early.

學習階段二Learning Stage 2

教師設計合適的學與教活動，監察和引導學生善用人工智能輔助學習，逐步認識人工智能的運作概念和特性，並培養學生以人為本、科技向善的精神，鼓勵他們養成慎用人工智能。

Teachers design suitable activities, and monitor and guide students to use AI well in support of learning. Students gradually learn how AI works and what it is like. Teachers nurture a human-centred spirit of technology for good, and encourage the habit of using AI with care.

03與計算思維、編程及資訊科技概念的關係How this connects with computational thinking, programming, and IT concepts

計算思維、編程及資訊科技概念幫助學生循序漸進地學習人工智能。學生通過編程或不插電活動拆解問題和設計解難方法與步驟，逐步掌握計算思維；而資訊科技知識亦有助他們理解數據處理與系統運作概念，這些學習經歷幫助他們學習和應用人工智能。

Computational thinking, programming, and IT concepts help students learn AI step by step. Through programming or unplugged activities, students break problems apart and design steps for solving them, and so build computational thinking. IT knowledge also helps them understand data processing and how systems work. These experiences help them learn and apply AI.

04持守以人為本、科技向善的精神Hold to a human-centred spirit: technology for good

在學習和應用人工智能時，教師需引導學生持守以人為本、科技向善的精神，運用合適而有效的課程設計和教學活動，培養學生正確使用人工智能的態度。學生必須：

When students learn and apply AI, teachers guide them to stay human-centred and to use technology for good. Through fitting and effective curriculum design and teaching activities, students build the right attitude to AI. Students must:

  1. 1

    認識人是主角：人工智能是人類的工具和助手，它是輔助學習與工作的工具。

    People stay in charge. AI is a human tool and assistant. It supports learning and work.

  2. 2

    培養判斷力：學會分辨甚麼時候需要人工智能協助完成工作，甚麼時候應該由自己完成工作，能夠適時適用人工智能。

    Build judgement. Learn when a task needs AI and when you should do it yourself, and use AI at the right time for the right job.

  3. 3

    先思而後用：做功課或解難時，先思考和嘗試，有需要時參考人工智能生成的內容。

    Think first, then use. When doing schoolwork or solving a problem, think and try first. Turn to AI-generated content when you need it.

  4. 4

    持續學習：要親自動手和動腦，不要濫用或完全依賴人工智能。

    Keep learning. Use your own hands and your own mind. Do not misuse AI or rely on it completely.

  5. 5

    認識創意來自人的想法：人工智能可以提供靈感，但真正的創意仍然來自人的想法。

    Creativity starts with people. AI can offer sparks of ideas, but real creativity still comes from a person’s own thinking.

  6. 6

    核實資訊真確性：要學會查證和核實資訊，人工智能提供的答案不一定全對，不應隨意使用或轉發未經核實的資訊。

    Check that information is true. Learn to verify what you read. AI answers are not always right. Do not use or pass on information you have not checked.

  7. 7

    道德地使用：合乎道德地運用人工智能，不要進行違反誠信和傷害別人的事，如抄襲、欺凌別人、詐騙。

    Use it ethically. Do not use AI to break trust or harm others, for example by plagiarising, bullying, or scamming.

  8. 8

    保護個人資料：不把自己和別人的個人資料輸入人工智能工具。

    Protect personal data. Do not enter your own or anyone else’s personal data into an AI tool.

  9. 9

    助己助人：善用人工智能幫助自己學習和解決問題，也可以通過互相分享和鼓勵，運用科技幫助他人和社群，推動科技向善。

    Help yourself and help others. Use AI well to learn and to solve problems. Share and encourage one another, and use technology to help other people and the community, so that technology is used for good.

06學習重點與學與教活動建議Learning focuses and suggested activities

以下列出各課題的學習重點及學與教活動建議。由於各學習重點互有連繫，學校需整體規劃課程和學與教安排。

Below are the learning focuses for each topic, with suggested learning and teaching activities. The focuses are connected, so schools should plan the curriculum and the arrangements as a whole.

A生活中的人工智能AI in everyday life

A小二 · P2

列舉生活中人工智能應用的例子Give examples of AI applications in everyday life

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 教師展示圖片或短片，介紹人工智能應用例子（如語音助理、臉部辨識）。The teacher shows pictures or short clips of AI applications, such as voice assistants and facial recognition.
  • 學生進行小組活動，以圖卡按「人工智能應用」（如語音助手、串流平台推薦影片功能、翻譯應用軟件）與「非人工智能應用」（如計算機、電子時鐘、簡單電腦遊戲）分類。In groups, students sort picture cards into “AI applications” (a voice assistant, video recommendations on a streaming platform, a translation app) and “non-AI applications” (a calculator, a digital clock, a simple computer game).
  • 學生分享「在我生活中的人工智能」。Students share “AI in my life”.
A小三 · P3

辨識非人工智能的資訊科技與人工智能的分別Tell non-AI information technology apart from AI

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 教師用生活例子作對比，講解兩者的分別（如計算機與語音助手）。The teacher contrasts everyday examples, such as a calculator and a voice assistant.
  • 學生進行角色扮演，模擬「程式」與「人工智能」的行為。Students role-play how a “program” behaves and how “AI” behaves.
  • 學生對非人工智能的資訊科技與人工智能作簡單比較，如「它是否會學習？」、「它是否能按情境的變化調整判斷？」Students make a simple comparison. For example: “Can it learn?” “Can it change its judgement when the situation changes?”
A小四 · P4

認識資訊科技和人工智能如何改善生活See how IT and AI can improve life

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 學生蒐集資訊科技和人工智能應用以提升工作質素和效率的例子。Students collect examples of IT and AI used to improve the quality and efficiency of work.
  • 學生分組，按不同領域和場景，討論資訊科技和人工智能技術如何提高工作或服務的質素和效率。In groups, students discuss how IT and AI raise the quality and efficiency of work or services in different fields and settings.
  • 教師以不同範疇的今昔對比，講述應用人工智能改善生活的場景，如人工智能輔助診斷和治療以提高醫療服務質素。The teacher compares past and present in different fields, and describes situations where AI improves life, such as AI-assisted diagnosis and treatment that raise the quality of healthcare.

B人工智能的能力與發展What AI can do, and how it has developed

B小二 · P2

簡單認識「智能」的概念（學習和作出決定）Gain a simple idea of “intelligence” (learning and making decisions)

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 教師運用日常情境講解學習和作出決定的關係，如根據天氣決定是否攜帶雨傘、根據交通燈指示決定應否過馬路，並說明人類能從經驗中學習並作出選擇。The teacher uses everyday situations to link learning and deciding: whether to take an umbrella because of the weather, or whether to cross because of the traffic light. People learn from experience and then choose.
  • 教師引導學生明白機器也可模仿這些能力，從而初步建立對「智能」及人工智能的基本概念。The teacher helps students see that machines can imitate these abilities, so they begin to form a basic idea of “intelligence” and of AI.
B小三 · P3

認識人工智能的基本能力和應用Know the basic abilities of AI and how they are used

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 教師引導學生認識人工智能功能和應用例子，如： The teacher introduces AI functions and examples, such as:
    • 以圖像辨識功能輔助搜尋圖像或辨識貨件Image recognition to search for pictures or identify parcels
    • 以語音辨識功能輔助語音輸入Speech recognition to support voice input
    • 以文字生成功能輔助寫作Text generation to support writing
    • 以影片生成功能輔助影片編輯與製作Video generation to support editing and producing videos
  • 學生根據所認識的人工智能功能，以繪畫和文字分享「我想要的人工智能助手」。Using the functions they have met, students draw and write about “the AI assistant I want”.
B小四 · P4

認識人工智能的演進，包括資訊和通訊科技的進步如何推動人工智能的發展Know how AI has developed, including how progress in information and communication technology has driven that development

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 學生嘗試列舉資訊和通訊科技的近代發展，如無線網絡、物聯網。Students try to name recent developments in information and communication technology, such as wireless networks and the Internet of Things.
  • 教師亦可向學生簡介一些與人工智能相關的新興科技，如大數據、雲計算、圖形處理器。The teacher may also briefly introduce emerging technologies linked to AI, such as big data, cloud computing, and graphics processing units.
  • 教師引導學生認識資訊和通訊科技進步與人工智能演進的關係，如物聯網和雲計算幫助數據收集和處理。The teacher helps students see the link. For example, the Internet of Things and cloud computing help with collecting and processing data.
B小五 · P5

初步認識影響人工智能效能的三大要素，包括數據、算法和算力Begin to know the three factors that affect how well AI performs: data, algorithms, and computing power

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 教師以問題帶出三大要素的重要性，如「為何有些人工智能工具比其他的更準確呢？」The teacher uses a question to show why the three factors matter, such as “Why are some AI tools more accurate than others?”
  • 教師以生活例子幫助學生理解數據、算法和算力，如以煮食為例：不同的食材（數據）、烹調步驟（算法）及爐火火力（算力），會影響菜式的質素。The teacher uses an everyday example. In cooking, the ingredients (data), the steps in the recipe (algorithms), and the heat of the stove (computing power) affect the quality of the dish.
  • 學生進行配對活動，就三大要素與例子作配對和簡單分析。Students match examples to the three factors and make a simple analysis.
B小五 · P5

認識人工智能如何收集數據Know how AI collects data

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 教師重溫學生對一般輸入裝置的認識（如網絡攝影機、麥克風），並討論日常收集數據的方法。The teacher revisits input devices students already know, such as webcams and microphones, and discusses everyday ways of collecting data.
  • 學生進行分類活動，討論哪些情況可能會被人工智能收集自己的數據（如瀏覽網頁、在社交媒體上留言），亦可延伸討論哪些數據會被收集。Students sort situations and discuss when AI might collect data about them, for example when they browse the web or comment on social media. They can go on to discuss which data would be collected.
  • 教師引導學生思考和關注私隱與數據安全。The teacher leads students to think about privacy and data security.
B小五 · P5

認識人工智能自動化Know about AI automation

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 學生列舉自動化的例子，如自動門、自動導航。Students give examples of automation, such as automatic doors and automated navigation.
  • 教師以生活場景或例子（如物流、交通），介紹人工智能自動化的應用。Using everyday settings such as logistics and transport, the teacher introduces uses of AI automation.

C初步體驗人工智能不同的應用A first experience of different AI applications

C小二 · P2

與人工智能系統進行簡單的「對話」，從中知道它能以文字、圖像和語音等形式作出回應Have a simple “conversation” with an AI system, and see that it can reply in text, images, speech, and other forms

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 教師提醒學生使用規則，如安全守則、合宜提問、避免過度使用。The teacher reminds students of the rules: safety, suitable questions, and not overusing the tool.
  • 教師運用提問句型練習卡，協助學生草擬與人工智能「對話」的內容，如「甚麼是…」、「請告訴我…」。Using question-pattern cards, the teacher helps students draft what to say, such as “What is …?” and “Please tell me …”.
  • 學生在教師指導下，按指定主題（如動物、交通）草擬「對話」內容，在教師指引和監督下與人工智能「對話」，並記錄其回應作匯報。With the teacher’s guidance, students draft a conversation on a set theme, such as animals or transport. Under the teacher’s direction and supervision they talk with the AI, record its replies, and report back.
C小三 · P3

知道人工智能可按要求生成圖像Know that AI can generate an image from a request

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 教師介紹提示詞的概念，並示範向人工智能輸入簡單指令以生成圖像。The teacher introduces prompts and demonstrates a simple instruction that generates an image.
  • 學生在教師指導下，運用指定主題和詞語草擬提示詞（如「貓 + 太空 + 卡通」→ 請繪畫一隻在太空的卡通貓）。With guidance, students draft a prompt from a given theme and words. For example, “cat + space + cartoon” becomes “Please draw a cartoon cat in space”.
  • 教師按提示詞主題把學生分組，讓學生互相比較提示詞和所生成的圖片。The teacher groups students by prompt theme so they can compare prompts and the pictures that were generated.
C小三 · P3

知道人工智能可按要求生成音訊Know that AI can generate audio from a request

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 教師示範使用文字生成語音工具。The teacher demonstrates a text-to-speech tool.
  • 學生在教師指導下輸入文字和修改語音設定（如語言、語速、語調），然後聆聽生成的語音。With guidance, students enter text, change the voice settings (language, speed, tone), and listen to the speech that is generated.
  • 學生比較在不同設定下所生成的語音。Students compare the speech generated under different settings.

D提示詞的運用Using prompts

D小四 · P4

理解並模仿簡單清晰的提示詞Understand simple, clear prompts, and follow their pattern

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 教師準備一些提示詞例子，讓學生選擇哪些提示詞能得到更好的回應，並分享原因（如是否清晰？有沒有要求？有沒有具體內容？）。The teacher prepares sample prompts. Students choose which ones get a better response and say why: Is it clear? Does it make a request? Does it include specific details?
  • 教師設定主題和準備提示詞例子，讓學生模仿和修訂，以生成文字或圖片，並初步判斷結果是否符合要求。（可包括各科的科本內容和要求。）The teacher sets a theme and provides sample prompts. Students copy and revise them to generate text or pictures, and make a first judgement about whether the result meets the request. Subject content and requirements may be included.
D小五 · P5

撰寫符合基本結構的完整提示詞Write a complete prompt with a basic structure

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 教師提供句式框架（如主題 + 角色 + 場景 + 風格）。學生按框架輸入提示詞，生成故事插圖、簡短描述或海報，並檢視生成內容如何配合提示詞。（可包括各科的科本內容和要求。）The teacher provides a frame, such as theme + role + setting + style. Students enter a prompt in that frame to generate a story illustration, a short description, or a poster, and check how the result follows the prompt. Subject content and requirements may be included.
D小六 · P6

自主編寫提示詞以完成任務Write prompts on their own to complete a task

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 學生因應不同任務（如宣傳海報、故事創作），自行設計提示詞，測試和檢視不同寫法對結果的影響，並作簡單修訂。（可包括各科的科本內容和要求。）For different tasks, such as a promotional poster or a story, students design their own prompts, test how different wording changes the result, and make simple revisions. Subject content and requirements may be included.

E數據與人工智能的「學習」Data and how AI “learns”

E小四 · P4

講述自己如何學習新知識，以及日常生活中如何通過資料和經驗作出判斷，從而初步認識機器學習的基本概念Describe how they learn something new, and how they judge from information and experience in daily life, and so meet the basic idea of machine learning

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 教師邀請學生分享「如何辨認這是蘋果？」和「如何辨認某位同學？」，引導學生回憶面對新面孔或新事物時，自己如何「學習」。The teacher invites students to share “How do I tell this is an apple?” and “How do I recognise a classmate?”, and helps them recall how they “learn” when they meet a new face or a new thing.
  • 教師準備一系列圖片或音訊，讓學生嘗試分析、辨識規律與特徵，從而體驗機器學習「從數據中學習，學習後懂得作出決定（如辨識）」的過程，了解其與人類學習的相似之處。The teacher prepares pictures or audio. Students look for patterns and features. They experience how machine learning “learns from data and can then make a decision, such as recognising something”, and see how this is similar to human learning.
E小五 · P5

運用數據「訓練」人工智能和測試其「訓練成果」Use data to “train” an AI and test what that “training” achieved

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 學生蒐集一定數量的動物或物件圖片，運用圖像機器學習平台，以所蒐集的圖片「訓練」人工智能。Students collect a set of pictures of animals or objects and use an image machine-learning platform to “train” an AI with those pictures.
  • 學生記錄與比較辨識結果。（可結合 STEAM 教育和專題研習進行學習。）Students record and compare the recognition results. This can be combined with STEAM education and project learning.
E小五 · P5

簡單分析數據如何影響人工智能「學習」Make a simple analysis of how data affects AI “learning”

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 承接「訓練」人工智能活動，教師請學生刻意蒐集偏差的數據（如以識別貓為目標，刻意找一些罕見品種的貓），再用新一組圖片「訓練」人工智能，以比較辨識結果，並進行簡單分析。Following the training activity, the teacher asks students to deliberately collect skewed data. If the goal is to recognise cats, they purposely find rare breeds. They “train” the AI again with a new set of pictures, compare the results, and make a simple analysis.
E小五 · P5

認識人類學習與機器學習的分別Know how human learning differs from machine learning

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 承接「訓練」人工智能活動，學生討論自己的學習過程與機器學習的分別。Following the training activity, students discuss how their own learning differs from machine learning.
  • 教師引導學生認識兩者的分別：機器按指示「學習」數據，較難應對「學習」以外的新情況；人類按動機來學習（如好奇心、求知慾），還會結合情感和道德判斷。A machine “learns” the data it is given and copes less well with new situations outside that. People learn from motives such as curiosity and the wish to know, and they also bring in feelings and moral judgement.

F識別與生成Recognition and generation

F小四 · P4

嘗試辨識人工智能生成的圖像、音頻和視像Try to recognise AI-generated images, audio, and video

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 教師準備一些圖像，請學生觀察和討論圖像是否由人工智能生成。教師需按學習目標適切選擇圖像： The teacher prepares images and asks students whether AI generated them. The images should fit the learning goal:
    • 讓學生認識辨別人工智能生成圖像的方法，如細節異常、背景不配合、場景不合理。Students learn signs of an AI-generated image, such as odd details, a background that does not fit, or a scene that does not make sense.
    • 讓學生意識到人工智能可以生成仿真度很高的圖像，一般人難以分辨，從而明白對網上的多媒體內容要保持質疑。Students realise AI can generate very realistic images that most people cannot tell apart, so they should keep questioning multimedia content online.
  • 教師可同時引入虛假資訊和深偽的討論。除圖像外，類同活動可以用在音頻和視像。The teacher may also open a discussion of false information and deepfakes. The same kind of activity can be used with audio and video, not only images.
F小四 · P4

運用人工智能識別圖像的內容Use AI to recognise what is in an image

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 學生體驗圖像辨識工具（如以人工智能平台辨識圖像），測試和評估準確度，並討論影響準確度的成因。Students try an image-recognition tool, test and assess its accuracy, and discuss what affects that accuracy.
F小五 · P5

運用人工智能識別語音的內容Use AI to recognise what is said in speech

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 學生體驗語音辨識工具（如以語音輸入文字），測試和評估準確度，並討論影響準確度的成因。Students try a speech-recognition tool, such as turning speech into text, test and assess its accuracy, and discuss what affects that accuracy.
F小五 · P5

認識人工智能識別圖像的基本概念Know the basic idea of how AI recognises images

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 學生進行認人或認物遊戲，運用特徵識別人或物（如長頸、斑點 → 長頸鹿）。Students play a recognition game and use features to identify a person or an object. For example, a long neck and spots point to a giraffe.
  • 教師簡介人工智能以物件特徵（如外形、顏色）進行識別。The teacher briefly explains that AI recognises objects by features such as shape and colour.
  • 作為延伸，教師與學生探討人工智能如何通過文字、圖像、音頻等識別使用者的「情感」。As an extension, the teacher and students explore how AI may use text, images, audio, and so on to identify a user’s “emotions”.
F小六 · P6

認識人工智能生成內容的基本概念Know the basic idea of how AI generates content

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 學生講述通過模仿而自我提升的學習經歷（如臨摹字帖習字、模仿新聞播報員的言語表達）。Students describe times they improved by copying a model, such as practising calligraphy from a copybook, or copying how a newsreader speaks.
  • 教師簡介人工智能通過處理大量數據進行「學習」，以生成內容，包括文字、影像和程式碼。The teacher briefly explains that AI “learns” by processing large amounts of data so it can generate content, including text, images, and program code.
F小六 · P6

適切地選擇和採用生成結果Choose and adopt generated results in a fitting way

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 教師引導學生建構評估生成內容的準則（如準確性、相關性）。（可包括各科的科本內容和要求。）The teacher helps students build criteria for judging generated content, such as accuracy and relevance. Subject content and requirements may be included.
  • 學生按準則評估不同的生成內容，並說明選擇或採用該內容的理由。Students use the criteria to assess different results and explain why they would choose or adopt a piece of content.

G人工智能的局限Limits of AI

G小六 · P6

明白人工智能有可能生成錯誤和偏差的結果或判斷Understand that AI may produce wrong or biased results or judgements

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 教師從日常生活中的「偏見」或錯誤判斷出發，引導學生討論人工智能的偏見與公平。Starting from “bias” or wrong judgements in everyday life, the teacher leads a discussion of AI bias and fairness.
  • 教師引導學生初步分析產生偏差的原因（如資料來源有限或不完整），並討論相關議題，如媒體與資訊素養、數據偏見、人工智能安全和人工智能幻覺。Students make a first analysis of why bias arises, for example when sources are limited or incomplete. They discuss media and information literacy, data bias, AI safety, and AI hallucination.
G小六 · P6

探討人類如何駕馭人工智能Explore how people can steer AI

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 教師引導學生明白駕馭和管理人工智能的需要。The teacher helps students see why people need to steer and manage AI.
  • 教師引導學生持守以人為本、科技向善的精神運用人工智能，包括以下原則： Students use AI in a human-centred way, with technology for good, including these principles:
    • 先思而後用，不要讓人工智能取代思考Think first, then use. Do not let AI replace thinking.
    • 配置合適的設定，規範人工智能的使用Put suitable settings in place to govern how AI is used.
    • 以合適的數據「訓練」人工智能“Train” AI with suitable data.
    • 監察和檢查人工智能的判斷和生成結果Monitor and check AI judgements and generated results.
    • 持守學術誠信Uphold academic integrity.
    • 不要過度依賴人工智能Do not rely on AI too much.
    • 負責任和符合道德地使用人工智能Use AI responsibly and ethically.

H善用人工智能Using AI well

H小二 · P2

認識過度依賴資訊科技和人工智能的不良影響Know the harm of relying too much on IT and AI

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 教師通過情境故事，引導學生討論過度依賴人工智能可能出現的後果，如思考能力和創造力減弱、影響腦部發展。Through a situation story, students discuss possible results of relying on AI too much, such as weaker thinking and creativity, and effects on how the brain develops.
  • 教師引用相關新聞報道或文章，提醒學生不要過度依賴人工智能。The teacher refers to news reports or articles and reminds students not to rely on AI too much.
H小三 · P3

建立避免依賴人工智能的意識Become aware of the need not to depend on AI

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 教師以合適的情境引導學生建立這份意識。例如面對不懂的功課，應先溫習和回顧所學，才向師長提問。The teacher uses a fitting situation. For example, when students do not understand a piece of homework, they first revise what they have learned, and then ask a teacher or an elder.
  • 教師引導學生明白學習過程比取得答案重要。The teacher helps students see that the process of learning matters more than getting the answer.
H小三 · P3

知道適合運用人工智能的情境Know when it is suitable to use AI

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 教師通過不同情境，引導學生思考是否適合使用人工智能，例如： Through different situations, students consider whether AI is suitable. For example:
    • 適合用來幫助學習（如協助學習新知識、搜尋資料、提供初步創作靈感）。Suitable for helping learning: learning something new, searching for information, or getting a first spark of an idea.
    • 不適合用來取代學習、違反學術誠信，或未經自己判斷便直接用人工智能做重要決定（如代做課業、作弊）。Not suitable for replacing learning, breaking academic integrity, or letting AI make an important decision before you have judged it yourself, such as doing your schoolwork for you or cheating.
H小四 · P4

認識在運用人工智能時可做與不可做的行為Know what you may and may not do when using AI

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 教師講解合乎道德地使用人工智能的守則（如不侵犯版權、不輸入自己或他人的個人資料），並通過情境分析正當和不當的使用行為。The teacher explains rules for using AI ethically, such as not infringing copyright and not entering your own or anyone else’s personal data, and uses situations to look at proper and improper use.
  • 學生製作「人工智能使用守則」海報或其他多媒體成品，宣傳正確使用人工智能的訊息。Students make a poster of rules for using AI, or another multimedia piece, to spread the message of using AI properly.
H小六 · P6

明白人機協作的概念，包括如何運用人工智能推動自主學習Understand human–AI collaboration, including how AI can support self-directed learning

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 教師引導學生運用人工智能協助查閱資料和整理重點。The teacher guides students to use AI to help look up information and organise key points.
  • 學生嘗試在人工智能輔助下完成學習任務，並反思人工智能如何提升學習效能。例如：「過程中我學到了甚麼？」「如何利用科本知識建構提示詞和查證生成內容？」「人工智能在學習過程中幫助了甚麼？」Students complete a learning task with AI support and reflect on how it helped. For example: “What did I learn along the way?” “How did I use subject knowledge to build the prompt and check the result?” “What did AI actually help with?”
  • 可配合其他科目應用人工智能的學習任務。目標集中於以人工智能輔助學習，而非取代學習和思考。This can be paired with tasks in other subjects. The aim is to use AI to support learning, not to replace learning and thinking.
H小六 · P6

運用人工智能設計和優化解決問題方案Use AI to design and improve a solution to a problem

學與教活動建議 Suggested activities

  • 教師安排專題式學習活動，讓學生綜合應用人工智能、編程和其他科目的知識構思方案（如智能校園或智能家居方案）。The teacher arranges project-based learning. Students draw on AI, programming, and other subjects to design a solution, such as a smart campus or a smart home.
  • 可結合 STEAM 教育和專題研習進行。This can be combined with STEAM education and project learning.
